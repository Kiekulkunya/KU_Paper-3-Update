**Gaps and limits**

- This is a high-confidence retrieval set, not verified exhaustive coverage. Several direct PDFs were identified but not fully text-extracted within the tool budget; those rows are marked as partial where applicable.
- Publication dates were not invented. Where only file paths, report years, meeting dates, or event dates were available, the date field states that limitation.
- Some ONWR, Budget Bureau, Royal Gazette and Cabinet materials may exist behind dynamic pages, cloud-storage links, Thai-only metadata, or non-indexed PDFs; unsuccessful retrieval here should not be interpreted as absence of policy activity.
- The requested period extends to 2026-09-13, but some retrieved sources referenced future-dated or post-cutoff file paths/events in the search index. Rows were retained only where they concerned the requested period or were indexed as available by the retrieval date; questionable future-dated records should be rechecked against source metadata before legal reliance.
- Page-specific citations are limited to passages surfaced by search/entity validation. A full audit-grade deliverable would require downloading each PDF, OCR where necessary, and page-by-page extraction.

**Excluded or deprioritized:** Corporate-only, municipal-only and SOE-only items returned during validation, including MEA/EGAT/BAAC-style green or sustainability bonds and ordinary non-labelled government bonds, were not retained unless the retrieved evidence explicitly documented a central-government sovereign programme connection. Some PDMO non-labelled bonds appeared in searches and were excluded from the main table.

**General government policy:** NDCs, LT-LEDS, NAP, BCG, Environmental Quality Management Plan, Disaster Prevention Plan, Water Master Plan and PM2.5 plan are general central-government policy/implementation documents. They are not treated as bond obligations unless a PDMO bond document explicitly connects them.

**Bond programme commitments:** The 2020 KOT Sustainable Financing Framework and SPO establish programme-level use-of-proceeds, project-selection, proceeds-management, allocation and impact-reporting commitments for sovereign sustainability financing; they do not make every national environmental policy a bond covenant.

**Security specific commitments:** ESGLB35DA is identified as a sovereign sustainability bond with use-of-proceeds characteristics. SLB406A is identified as a sovereign sustainability-linked bond; sustainability-linked obligations differ from green/sustainability use-of-proceeds obligations and should not be assumed equivalent.
